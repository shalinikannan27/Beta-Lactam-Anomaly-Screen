Undergraduate Research Context

This project represents the culmination of a multi-semester undergraduate research initiative focused on the intersection of bioinformatics and clinical microbiology. Our goal was to create an accessible yet rigorous tool for predicting phenotypic resistance from genotypic data.

By leveraging state-of-the-art machine learning models and high-throughput sequencing data, we aim to reduce the time-to-insight for clinicians faced with multidrug-resistant pathogens.
