The Clinical Context

Historically, S. pyogenes (Group A Streptococcus) has remained remarkably susceptible to penicillin and other β-lactam antibiotics. However, the global landscape is shifting.

Emerging genomic data reveals that specific pbp2x mutations are being detected in clinical isolates worldwide. These mutations represent a pre-resistance state that could potentially compromise the efficacy of standard first-line treatments.
