Legal & Research Disclaimer

The AMR Atlas is a specialized computational framework designed for high-throughput genomic analysis of antimicrobial resistance. Its use is strictly governed by the following research and clinical limitations.

science

Research Use Only (RUO)

AMR Atlas is explicitly designated for experimental and educational research purposes. The predictive models and genetic markers identified within this platform are the result of ongoing genomic investigation and have not been cleared by any regulatory health authority (such as the FDA or EMA) for commercial or diagnostic use.

STATUS: NON-DIAGNOSTIC / EXPERIMENTAL

medical_information

Not Clinical Advice

Nothing on this platform should be construed as medical diagnosis, treatment recommendations, or clinical guidance. Genomic predictions for drug resistance are probabilistic and must not supersede established clinical susceptibility testing (AST) or the professional judgment of a qualified medical practitioner.

clinical_notes

Consult a Professional

Users are strongly advised to consult with board-certified microbiologists, infectious disease specialists, or genomic counselors before drawing conclusions from analysis results. Any action taken based on the data provided by AMR Atlas is strictly at the user's own risk.

error

Accuracy Limitations

AMR prediction accuracy is highly dependent on the quality of input sequences and the current state of genomic databases.

Bioinformatic Variability

False positives and false negatives may occur due to sequencing errors, horizontal gene transfer, or novel mutation variants not yet cataloged.

Model Drift

Predictive weights are updated periodically and may change as new clinical phenotypes are correlated with genomic signatures.

Data Privacy

Our platform prioritizes the security of your genomic metadata. While we implement industry-standard encryption, users are responsible for ensuring that uploaded sequences do not contain Personally Identifiable Information (PII) or Protected Health Information (PHI).
